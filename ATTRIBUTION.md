# Music Attribution

The starter/vetted tracks that ship with or are fetched by this project, and the
credit each license requires. Run [`scripts/fetch-cc-music.sh`](scripts/fetch-cc-music.sh)
to add the Creative-Commons / public-domain batch to `music/`.

> **If you broadcast the CC BY tracks below, you MUST credit them.** The on-stream
> now-playing overlay ("Kevin MacLeod — Cattails") is a start, but to satisfy
> CC BY put a standing credit where viewers can see it — your channel's **pinned
> post or bio** is the standard place. CC0 / public-domain tracks need no credit
> (thanking the artist is a kindness, not a requirement).
